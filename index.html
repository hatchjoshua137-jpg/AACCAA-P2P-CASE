Export for empowOR

Import files match empowOR's sample layouts. Import Households & Participants first, run Group Enrollment on that batch to enroll participants in "Pathway to Prosperity", then import Income, Case Notes, and Enrollment & Services. IDs and the import source stay consistent across files.

How this tool works

Each tab follows the Pathway to Prosperity Operating Tools Packet. Everything saves as you type; there is no Save button. Green dots on the tabs show which parts are complete. Connect the SharePoint backup folder once on each laptop and every save is backed up automatically with version history.

The rules the tool enforces

  • Initial contact is due within two business days of the enrollment inquiry. The case card turns amber when it is overdue.
  • A substantive contact is expected weekly. Seven or more days without a progress note flags the case.
  • Any letter, email, or report to the court, defense counsel, the State's Attorney, or a supervision agency is drafted here, sent to the CEASO for approval, and cannot be marked as sent until the disposition is Approved.
  • A correspondence request cannot be submitted to a recipient the participant's signed authorization does not cover.
  • Referrals are tracked to connection and outcome, not just issuance.

Confidentiality

  • Participant ID only in file names, emails, and texts. Names stay inside this tool and the paper record.
  • No Social Security numbers, account numbers, or card numbers anywhere in the system.
  • Behavioral health and substance use: record referral status and connection only, never clinical detail.
  • Court and case information carries its source. Public case information is verified, not assumed.
  • Lock your screen when you step away. Every action in a case is logged.

Boundaries (from the Operating Procedures)

  • Staff do not give legal advice, predict case outcomes, or advocate for a specific sentence. Progress summaries state facts and program participation.
  • Staff attend court only with supervisor approval and only for a defined purpose.
  • Nothing goes to any justice-system party without the participant's signed authorization and CEASO approval.
